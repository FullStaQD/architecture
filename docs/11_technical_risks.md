# Risks and Technical Debts {#section-technical-risks}
This section describes the risks to the FullStaQD reference architecture, the countermeasures we implement, and the likelihood and strength of influence of each risk.

| Risk  | Countermeasure |   Likelihood | Impact |  
| ------------- | ------------- |   :---: |   :---:  | 
| Impact of New Research Findings on Future Project Work. | By continuously taking new publications into account, maintaining close ties with research institutions, industry, and other initiatives, and regularly participating in conferences, we ensure that relevant innovations are identified early on and can be incorporated as needed.  | middle | middle |
| Delays in the process, such as during the implementation of components.   |  A schedule is intended to help prevent delays in the completion of work packages. As part of overall project management, comprehensive communication and monitoring structures will also be established. Regular coordination meetings will be scheduled to review the status of the work packages. This will allow any problems to be identified and communicated early on, thereby accelerating the process of finding solutions within the consortium. | middle | low |
| Interdependencies among the work packages, including the integration of knowledge from interdisciplinary and transdisciplinary perspectives  |The project office will actively support knowledge transfer within the consortium within the defined governance structures. Cross-focus-group workshops and coordination meetings will ensure a continuous flow of information  | middle | middle |
| Scope Creep: Exceeding the originally agreed-upon project scope due to additional deliverables requested  | The clearly defined work packages enable targeted work based on the project schedule. Regular coordination meetings within the consortium, as well as with the project sponsor and funding agency, and the monitoring of the work packages make it possible to follow the project schedule in a targeted manner and take corrective action early on if necessary. | low | middle |
| Scarce resources, absence of individual stakeholders (change of employer, illness, bankruptcy)  | Given the ambitious objectives, the involvement of highly qualified staff and key stakeholders is an effective way to use scarce resources efficiently and effectively and to avoid deviations from the goals. A clear description of the project goal, the approach, and the division of labor also makes it possible to successfully onboard new colleagues as needed.    | middle | middle |
